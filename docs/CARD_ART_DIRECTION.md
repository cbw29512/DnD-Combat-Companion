# Monster card artwork contract

## Default
Use distinctive monster silhouettes as the default artwork for monster flip cards, including every edition. Prefer repository-owned or properly licensed silhouette assets; do not assume art from an external project is automatically reusable without checking rights.

## Card hierarchy
Front: silhouette, monster name, CR or edition-appropriate level, type, concise AC/HP, one prominent ATTACK button and large roll results.
Back: readable attack descriptions, traits, abilities, save DCs, and detailed rules. DM determines hits, saves, targets, and applied damage.

## Other cards
Spell cards use restrained fantasy-themed artwork or effect symbols. Condition and damage-type cards use clear, consistent icons.

## Performance/accessibility
Keep images compressed and appropriately sized, provide meaningful alternative text or mark decorative art as decorative, reserve card layout to avoid shifts, support reduced motion, and ensure cards remain usable if artwork fails to load.

## Implementation status
This is an accepted design rule, not a claim that silhouette assets have already been integrated into the live website.
